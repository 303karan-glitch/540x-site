Studio & people

At 540X, our journey began with the shared vision of five architects, each bringing unique expertise and perspectives to the table. We set out to craft spaces that reflect innovation, timelessness, and human connection. Our approach is rooted in collaboration, fostering a dynamic work environment where creativity thrives, fresh ideas are consistently encouraged, and innovation stays an integral part of our studio ensuring that our work is not only driven by creativity but also guided by a shared commitment to excellence.

At the core of our practice, we blend a dedication to structural clarity with a passion for infusing life and vitality into spaces. We seamlessly integrate refined aesthetic sensibilities with a deep understanding of materials and details, striving to create environments that resonate with timeless quality and human-centric design. As a multidisciplinary practice, we explore architecture’s role in enhancing the quality of life.

Principal partners

Karmaraj Rana

Co-Founder & Principal Architect

Read bio

Ar. Karmaraj Rana is an accomplished architect with a B.Arch degree from IPSA, Rajkot. His architectural philosophy is firmly grounded in the authentic expression of materials and structural articulation, which are central to his design approaches. He is a strong advocate of clean aesthetics and skillfully orchestrates the interplay of volumes and spaces to create both clear and purposeful designs. Committed to collaboration, Karmaraj leads a dedicated team of designers, all united by a deep passion for exploring and refining architectural expressions through practice.

Abhishek Patel

Co-Founder & Principal Architect

Read bio

Ar. Abhishek Patel is a skilled architect and interior designer with expertise spanning residential, institutional, and commercial projects. His design philosophy reflects a commitment to sustainability and a deep appreciation for nature. With a focus on thoughtful integration of form and function, Abhishek brings a sense of balance and purpose to every project, fostering designs that inspire and endure.

Zenil Mahendi Ali

Co-Founder & Principal Designer

Read bio

Ar. Zenil Mahendi Ali is a talented architect with a B.Arch degree from the Indubhai Parekh School of Architecture. Her journey into architecture is deeply rooted in her passion for art, culture, and the transformative power of ideas. An advocate of lifelong learning, Zenil continually explores how architecture shapes identities, thoughts, and environments. Zenil’s creative pursuits extend beyond architecture to sculpting, painting, and eco-printing, reflecting her artistic sensibilities. With an eye for detail and a love for cultural exploration, Zenil’s work embodies innovation and artistic depth, enriching spaces with meaning and vitality.

Jeet Gajera

Co-Founder & Principal Architect

Read bio

Ar. Jeet Gajera is a distinguished architect with a B.Arch degree from IPSA, Rajkot. His deep commitment to crafting timeless architecture is complemented by his unique perspective as both an architect and furniture designer. His meticulous attention to detail and expertise in colour, finishes, and materials are evident in his works, which thoughtfully add sensorially rich experiences to spaces. His designs are characterized by their ability to infuse life and vitality into environments, creating enduring and meaningful architectural expressions.

Karan Solanki

Co-Founder & Creative Head

Read bio

Karan is an India-based architect and visual artist. Through an emotional approach to architecture, he is known for his bold, expressive use of space and form, often merging modernism with classical influences. His designs emphasize the relationship between architecture and human experience, aiming to evoke feelings and emotions in the people who interact with his buildings. His philosophy is rooted in creating a dialogue between architecture, history, and nature, and his works often feature monumental forms and the use of symbolic elements, merging the past with modernist sensibilities. With an experience of five years in architectural design & visualisation, Karan has published works on popular architecture websites like Archdaily and ThinkMatter Archives.

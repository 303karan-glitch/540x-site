Revah - Branding & Identity Design

The logo and visual language we developed for Studio Revah represent a journey, one that echoes the rhythm of life and constant evolution. We worked closely with the team to ensure that their brand identity would stand out in the design world—while remaining grounded in authenticity and meaningful connection. Our collaboration with Studio Revah reflects our commitment to designing brands that not only look striking but also communicate their deeper essence effectively.

A design studio inspired by the fluidity and continuity of the Narmada River. Our approach was to create a visual identity that embodied the essence of movement, harmony, and timelessness. Through strategic design, we reflected the studio’s philosophy of fluidity, ensuring that every element of the brand resonated with its core values of creativity and purposeful design.

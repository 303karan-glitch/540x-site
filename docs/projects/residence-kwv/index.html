KWV – A Weekend Retreat

Situated on a 500-var plot near Rajkot, KWV is a weekend home crafted for a family of three, with ample space to host guests. This farmhouse brings together the raw textures of concrete, stone, glass, and wood, creating spaces that feel grounded yet effortlessly elegant.

KWV offers three bedrooms, an activity room, a gym, and a glass outhouse, complemented by an organic-shaped swimming pool, a small tennis court, and versatile outdoor areas.

From a jacuzzi under a pitched roof to a shaded space with traditional jhulas and a tranquil viewing deck, every corner encourages relaxation and connection.

The design avoids excessive ornamentation, focusing instead on honest materials and functional simplicity. It’s a home that embraces the outdoors and lets the natural surroundings take the lead—a true retreat from the everyday.

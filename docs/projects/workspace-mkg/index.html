Located in the rapidly developing area of Morbi, Gujarat, MKG Office is designed as a compact yet functional workspace for two young entrepreneurs. The office layout balances efficiency and privacy, incorporating a head office, staff workspace, finance office, and a welcoming reception area.

The design emphasizes light, airy spaces with a seamless hierarchy, where privacy is maintained through subtle transitions, such as fluted glass partitions, creating a sense of openness while preserving boundaries.

A modern material palette defines the interiors, featuring soft Kota green tiles, pristine white walls, and modest ceilings. Fluted laminates, wooden backdrops, and upholstery highlight the texture and warmth, while stone tabletops with edge molding ensure ergonomic comfort.

With its minimal approach, clean lines, and thoughtfully composed forms, MKG Office is a refined and functional space, tailored for contemporary workstyles.

Studio Seaform – Visual Identity

The Studio Seaform logo and brand vision reflect the interplay between movement and form—capturing the transient beauty of waves as they take shape upon the shore. The bold yet fluid typography in "SF" mirrors the rhythm of the sea, embodying the essence of ideas taking form through visualization.

Studio Seaform embodies the fluidity of thought and the ever-changing nature of creativity. Inspired by the sea—its rhythm, its depth, and the fleeting forms it takes upon the shore—the visual identity captures moments of stillness within constant motion. It is a reflection of how ideas evolve, transform, and leave an imprint, much like waves shaping the coastline.

The three bedroom apartment was designed prioritizing functionality over excessive ornamentation. A minimalist approach was adopted to maximize the budget, focusing on intricate details that enhance usability. The material palette features warm steamed beech laminated wooden surfaces creating a cozy atmosphere, contrasted with earthy maroon limestone. Vibrant and luxurious upholstery adds richness to the space while maintaining simplicity.

The overall aesthetic embodies the elegant and minimalist Japandi style, seamlessly blended with elements of transitional design to achieve a balance of spaciousness and coziness.

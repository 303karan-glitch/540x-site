Located amidst the vibrant landscape of Jalgaon, Maharashtra, the Science Centre is a beacon for curiosity-driven minds and a platform for engaging the community with the wonders of science and technology. The thoughtfully designed campus harmonizes modern architectural elements with natural landscapes, creating a space where learning, discovery, and cultural interaction come to life.

The centre houses a dynamic array of facilities, including a research centre, an exhibition and museum space, a fully equipped auditorium for conferences, and a futuristic planetarium. The architectural language emphasizes structural clarity and material authenticity, offering an immersive experience both indoors and outdoors. The amphitheatre, designed as a social gathering hub, invites people to connect, reflect, and share ideas under the open sky.

Interactive digital exhibits and installations engage visitors across all age groups, transforming abstract scientific concepts into tangible experiences. Sustainability and a connection with the surrounding environment play a central role in the design, making it not just a hub for scientific learning but also an architectural landmark that integrates seamlessly into its natural context.

The Science Centre in Jalgaon aspires to nurture innovation, spark creativity, and bridge the gap between science and society, making it a transformative space for generations to come.

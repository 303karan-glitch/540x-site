Residence RKA – A Symphony of Marble and Wood

Residence RKA is a thoughtfully crafted 1180 sq ft home where refined details and a harmonious material palette create a distinctive living experience. The design celebrates the timeless beauty of marble, which lends a sense of sophistication to the interiors, while warm wooden elements add depth and comfort. Every corner has been meticulously detailed to enhance functionality while maintaining a seamless visual flow.

The residence strikes a perfect balance between elegance and warmth, offering an environment that feels both luxurious and inviting. Custom-crafted elements and tactile finishes further elevate the spatial experience, creating a home that resonates with the client’s desire for understated sophistication and thoughtful living.

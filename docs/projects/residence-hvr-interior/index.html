Nirvana Home is an ideal living space for our family—my wife, my retired parents, and myself. The design brief was simple: to create a light, airy, and minimalistic environment that is both functional and easy to maintain, while maximizing the enjoyment of the top-floor breeze in our new 14-story apartment.

This project is intended to enhance daily living and provide an inviting space for hosting gatherings. The design incorporates elegant elements such as brass pipe, steel’s bed, marble accents, and polished plywood, creating a harmonious blend of form and function. The plain white ceilings and walls serve as a backdrop, allowing the furniture, fabrics, and decor to take center stage.

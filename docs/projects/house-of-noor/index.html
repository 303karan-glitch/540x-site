Brand Design - Clothing & Apparels

House of Noor is a label born from a shared love for fabric, craft, and culture. Rooted in India’s rich heritage of textile artistry, the brand pays homage to skilled artisans who preserve and refine these timeless traditions. The identity reflects this ethos—blending elegance with authenticity to create a visual language that feels both modern and deeply connected to its origins.

Our branding approach captures the essence of Noor—light, grace, and craftsmanship—through refined typography, a warm, sophisticated color palette, and symbolic elements that echo the delicate intricacies of handwoven textiles. House of Noor stands as a bridge between heritage and contemporary fashion, celebrating the soul of tradition in every detail.

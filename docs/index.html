540X is a collaborative design practice founded by five principal partners, offering expertise in architecture, interior design, and visual narratives. Engaging in experiential architecture and timeless designs, we intend to craft spaces that redefine their surroundings, focusing on space-making, humane environments, and meaningful impact.

Since its inception, 540X has crafted high-end residential, Institutional, and hospitality spaces with a focus on timeless designs enriched with local influences. By blending the fresh perspectives of young minds with seasoned expertise, 540X continuously pushes the boundaries of design, creating environments that inspire and endure. With its roots in India and aspirations that extend globally, 540X remains dedicated to shaping spaces that are timeless, meaningful, and sustainable.

How we work

  1. 01

    Experience

    Spaces are shaped by the stories they hold. We design to evoke emotions and craft moments.

  2. 02

    Interactive Spaces

    Design is a dialogue between space and its users. Our work invites interaction, fostering connections through thoughtful layouts and purposeful design elements.

  3. 03

    Details

    Details are where design transforms into art. They are the silent narrators, shaping how a space feels, functions, and endures. It is through these intricacies that the ordinary becomes extraordinary.

  4. 04

    Playful

    Design can be serious, but it doesn’t have to feel that way. We infuse a sense of playfulness, using unexpected forms, vibrant colours, and unique ideas to add joy and personality to every project.

  5. 05

    Articulation

    Precision is the hallmark of great design. We articulate our vision with clarity, ensuring every line, material, and space comes together harmoniously to tell a cohesive and compelling story.

  6. 06

    Climate

    Sustainability starts with understanding. By embracing the local climate, culture, and environment, we create designs that are responsive, respectful, and built to last.

  7. 07

    Context

    We exist in a larger narrative. By understanding environment, culture, and purpose, we design in harmony with the surroundings in mind, ensuring the space feels grounded yet timeless.
